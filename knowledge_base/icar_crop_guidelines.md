# ICAR & Agricultural Benchmark Guidelines: 22 Major Crops

This reference guide documents recommended agro-climatic envelopes, optimal soil macronutrient levels (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium in kg/ha), soil pH tolerance bands, and ideal temperature/humidity/rainfall regimes across India's principal agricultural zones.

---

## 1. Cereal & Grain Crops

### Rice (*Oryza sativa*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 80 kg/ha N, 48 kg/ha P₂O₅, 40 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.5 – 6.8 (tolerant to slightly acidic submerged soils)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 22°C – 32°C; requires high relative humidity (>80%) and 150 – 300 mm monthly rainfall during vegetative and reproductive stages.
- **Agronomic Key**: Anaerobic root zone dynamics favor ammonium-based nitrogen sources. Flooded paddy soils buffer pH variations, but excessive acidity (<5.0) induces iron and aluminum toxicity.

### Maize (*Zea mays*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 78 kg/ha N, 48 kg/ha P₂O₅, 20 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.8 – 7.2 (well-drained loam or alluvial soils)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 20°C – 28°C; moderate humidity (60% – 75%); rainfall 60 – 100 mm during silking.
- **Agronomic Key**: Extremely sensitive to waterlogging. High early-stage nitrogen demand for canopy expansion and grain filling.

## 2. Pulse & Legume Crops

### Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 40 kg/ha N, 68 kg/ha P₂O₅, 80 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.5 – 8.0 (neutral to slightly alkaline light loams)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 15°C – 25°C; low humidity (15% – 30%); rainfall 60 – 100 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: High phosphorus and potassium requirement stimulates *Rhizobium* root nodulation, reducing synthetic N necessity.

### Kidneybeans (*Phaseolus vulgaris*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 21 kg/ha N, 67 kg/ha P₂O₅, 20 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.5 – 6.5 (moderately acidic to neutral)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 18°C – 24°C; humidity 50% – 65%; rainfall 90 – 130 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Weak symbiotic N-fixation compared to other legumes; benefits from basal P application.

### Pigeonpeas (*Cajanus cajan*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 21 kg/ha N, 68 kg/ha P₂O₅, 20 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.5 – 7.5 (deep alluvial or black cotton soils)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 25°C – 35°C; humidity 45% – 60%; rainfall 120 – 180 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Deep taproot enables drought tolerance; thrives with split phosphate topdressing.

### Mothbeans (*Vigna aconitifolia*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 21 kg/ha N, 48 kg/ha P₂O₅, 20 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.5 – 8.2 (arid, sandy to sandy-loam soils)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 26°C – 35°C; humidity 45% – 65%; rainfall 40 – 70 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: High tolerance to extreme heat and drought; requires minimal synthetic fertilizer inputs.

### Mungbean (*Vigna radiata*) & Blackgram (*Vigna mungo*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 21 – 40 kg/ha N, 47 – 67 kg/ha P₂O₅, 20 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.2 – 7.5
- **Temperature & Climate**: 25°C – 32°C; rainfall 50 – 80 mm; moderate humidity.
- **Agronomic Key**: Short duration (60-75 days) crops. Ideal catch crops that enrich soil nitrogen via biological fixation.

### Lentil (*Lens culinaris*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 19 kg/ha N, 68 kg/ha P₂O₅, 19 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.5
- **Temperature & Climate**: 18°C – 26°C; rainfall 40 – 60 mm; cold and dry winter conditions.
- **Agronomic Key**: Highly susceptible to waterlogging; demands loose, well-aerated seedbeds.

## 3. Commercial & Fiber Crops

### Cotton (*Gossypium hirsutum*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 118 kg/ha N, 46 kg/ha P₂O₅, 19 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.5 – 8.0 (deep black soils/Vertisols with high clay content)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 22°C – 32°C; humidity 65% – 85%; rainfall 60 – 110 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: High vegetative N consumption followed by potassium demand during boll development to ensure fiber length and strength.

### Jute (*Corchorus olitorius*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 78 kg/ha N, 46 kg/ha P₂O₅, 40 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.5 (alluvial loams in eastern river basins)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 24°C – 35°C; humidity >75%; rainfall 150 – 250 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Requires abundant moisture and warm conditions to sustain rapid fibrous stem growth.

### Coffee (*Coffea arabica / canephora*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 101 kg/ha N, 29 kg/ha P₂O₅, 30 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 6.8 (forest loam rich in organic matter)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 20°C – 28°C; humidity 55% – 75%; rainfall 140 – 200 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Shade-grown plantation crop; requires balanced potassium for berry development and quality bean density.

## 4. Fruit & Horticultural Crops

### Banana (*Musa acuminata*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 100 kg/ha N, 82 kg/ha P₂O₅, 50 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.5 (deep, well-drained loams rich in humus)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 25°C – 32°C; humidity >75%; rainfall 100 – 180 mm monthly.
- **Agronomic Key**: Extremely heavy consumer of nutrients and soil moisture; potassium drives bunch weight and sugar content.

### Mango (*Mangifera indica*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 20 kg/ha N, 27 kg/ha P₂O₅, 30 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.5 – 7.2
- **Temperature & Climate**: 24°C – 34°C; requires a distinct dry period prior to flowering.
- **Agronomic Key**: Excess nitrogen during flowering causes vegetative flushing and flower drop.

### Grapes (*Vitis vinifera*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 23 kg/ha N, 133 kg/ha P₂O₅, 201 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.5 (sandy to gravelly loam with excellent drainage)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 20°C – 30°C; low humidity (<60%) during berry ripening; rainfall 50 – 80 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Heavy potassium demand for sugar accumulation and berry skin resilience.

### Watermelon & Muskmelon (*Citrullus lanatus / Cucumis melo*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 99 – 100 kg/ha N, 17 – 18 kg/ha P₂O₅, 50 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.0 (sandy loam with high thermal capacity)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 24°C – 32°C; rainfall 25 – 60 mm; low humidity during ripening.
- **Agronomic Key**: Avoid overhead wetting to prevent fungal rot. High initial nitrogen followed by potassium for sweetness.

### Apple (*Malus domestica*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 21 kg/ha N, 134 kg/ha P₂O₅, 200 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.8 – 6.8 (deep, well-aerated temperate mountain soils)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 15°C – 24°C; chilling hours requirement in winter; rainfall 100 – 130 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: High phosphorus for root establishment and potassium for fruit color and firmness.

### Orange & Citrus (*Citrus sinensis*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 20 kg/ha N, 16 kg/ha P₂O₅, 10 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.5
- **Temperature & Climate**: 20°C – 30°C; moderate humidity; rainfall 90 – 130 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Sensitive to soil salinity and waterlogging; demands good subsoil drainage.

### Papaya (*Carica papaya*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 50 kg/ha N, 59 kg/ha P₂O₅, 50 kg/ha K₂O (Per-plant dosage: 200–250 g N, 200–250 g P₂O₅, 400–500 g K₂O per year applied in 6 bimonthly splits).
- **Soil pH & Reaction**: 6.0 – 7.2 (Loamy, alluvial or well-drained red/black soils). Highly sensitive to acidic soils (pH < 6.0); requires 1.2–1.5 t/ha agricultural lime (CaCO₃) to neutralize toxic aluminum/iron ions and prevent damping-off.
- **Hydrological & Drainage Needs**: Extremely intolerant to water stagnation or waterlogging. Standing water for even 24–48 hours induces fatal collar rot / foot rot (*Pythium / Phytophthora*). Must be cultivated on raised beds (30–45 cm height) or ridge-and-furrow systems with active drainage channels.
- **Temperature & Climate**: 25°C – 35°C; relative humidity >70%; rainfall 120 – 160 mm. Fruit setting drops below 16°C or above 38°C.
- **Irrigation & Water Balance (FAO-56)**: $K_{c\text{, ini}} = 0.50$, $K_{c\text{, mid}} = 1.00$, $K_{c\text{, end}} = 0.85$. Rooting depth $Z_r = 0.6 - 1.0\text{ m}$, depletion fraction $p = 0.40$. Drip irrigation with ring emitters is strongly recommended to keep water away from direct contact with the stem collar.
- **Spacing & Planting Density**: 1.8 m x 1.8 m (1,200 plants/acre) or 2.1 m x 2.1 m (900 plants/acre) in pits of 60 x 60 x 60 cm filled with topsoil, 20 kg FYM, 1 kg neem cake, and 250 g SSP.
- **Commercial Varieties**: Red Lady 786 (gynodioecious, uniform fruit size, sweet aroma), Taiwan 786, Pusa Delicious, Pusa Dwarf, Coorg Honey Dew.
- **Key Diseases & Management**:
  - *Collar Rot / Stem Rot (Pythium aphanidermatum, Phytophthora nicotianae)*: Soil drenching around collar with Metalaxyl-Mancozeb (Ridomil MZ @ 2 g/L) or Copper Oxychloride (@ 3 g/L). Maintain elevated soil mounds around trunk base.
  - *Papaya Ring Spot Virus (PRSV)*: Vector-transmitted by aphids (*Aphis gossypii*). Plant 2–3 rows of barrier crops (maize or sorghum) around field borders; spray Imidacloprid (0.3 ml/L) or Neem oil (3 ml/L) at first aphid sightings.
  - *Root Knot Nematode (Meloidogyne incognita)*: Incorporate 25 g *Trichoderma viride* / *Paecilomyces lilacinus* per pit at planting.
- **Commercial Economics & Yield**:
  - Yield: 25 – 35 tonnes / acre (60 – 80 kg per plant).
  - Wholesale Mandi Price: ₹1,500 – ₹2,800 / Quintal.
  - Estimated Gross Revenue: ₹1,80,000 – ₹2,80,000 / acre.
  - Cultivation Time: 9 – 12 months for initial fruit maturity; economic orchard lifespan 2 – 3 years.
- **Agronomic Key**: Continuous feeder throughout the vegetative and fruit-bearing period. Ensure continuous organic matter (FYM) and potash top-dressing to maintain high fruit Brix (>12° Brix sweetness).


### Coconut (*Cocos nucifera*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 22 kg/ha N, 17 kg/ha P₂O₅, 31 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 5.2 – 8.0 (tolerant to coastal sands and alluvium)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 24°C – 32°C; humidity >70%; rainfall 150 – 220 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: High chlorine and potassium uptake essential for nut water retention and copra yield.

### Pomegranate (*Punica granatum*)
- **Optimal Soil NPK**: 19 kg/ha N, 19 kg/ha P₂O₅, 40 kg/ha K₂O
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 – 7.5 (light loamy soils)
- **Temperature & Climate**: 20°C – 32°C; semi-arid preference; rainfall 80 – 120 mm.
- **Agronomic Key**: Withstand drought, but requires regular drip irrigation during fruit swelling to prevent fruit cracking.
