**Farmer Query**:
> What is the FAO-56 root zone depletion formula (Dr,i) from the water balance manual?

**Kisan AI Answer**:
📘 **FAO-56 Irrigation Water Balance Model (Equation 85)**

According to the **FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56**, root zone depletion ($D_{r,i}$) at the end of day $i$ is calculated via the daily soil water balance:

$$\mathbf{D_{r,i} = D_{r,i-1} - (P - RO)_i - I_i - CR_i + ET_{c,i} + DP_i}$$

**Parameter Definitions:**
- **$D_{r,i}$**: Root zone depletion at the end of day $i$ [mm]
- **$D_{r,i-1}$**: Depletion at the end of the previous day $i-1$ [mm]
- **$P_i$**: Total rainfall / precipitation on day $i$ [mm]
- **$RO_i$**: Runoff loss from the soil surface on day $i$ [mm]
- **$I_i$**: Net irrigation depth that infiltrates the root zone [mm]
- **$CR_i$**: Capillary rise from shallow groundwater [mm]
- **$ET_{c,i}$**: Daily crop evapotranspiration ($ET_o \times K_c$) [mm/day]
- **$DP_i$**: Deep percolation water loss past root zone boundary [mm]

**Irrigation Decision Rule:**
Irrigation is triggered when $D_{r,i} \ge RAW$, where **Readily Available Water** $RAW = p \times TAW$.
