--- TOMATO (Lycopersicon esculentum) ---
Scientific Name:   Lycopersicon esculentum / Solanum lycopersicum
Family:            Solanaceae
Season:            Kharif (June-Sep), Rabi (Oct-Feb), Summer (Feb-May)
Optimal pH:        6.0 - 7.5
Optimal N (kg/ha): 100 - 150
Optimal P (kg/ha): 50 - 80
Optimal K (kg/ha): 60 - 80
Min N Threshold:   60
Min P Threshold:   40
Min K Threshold:   40
Temperature:       20 - 30 °C (Optimal germination: 22-28°C)
Max Temp Tolerance:35 °C (Fruit set drops above 35°C)
Moisture:          40 - 70% (Regular drip irrigation preferred)
Min Moisture:      35%
Kc_mid:            1.15
Kc_end:            0.70 - 0.90
Max Height:        0.6 - 1.5 m (staked varieties)
Root Depth:        0.7 - 1.5 m
Spacing:           60x45 cm (open field), 45x30 cm (protected)
Key Pests:         Fruit borer (Helicoverpa armigera), whitefly, leaf curl virus
Soil Amendment:    Acidic soils (<6.0): Apply 1.0-1.5 t/ha agricultural lime
                   Alkaline soils (>7.5): Apply 200 kg/acre gypsum
Fertilizer Split:  50% N basal + 25% at 30 DAS + 25% at flowering
                   100% P basal + 50% K basal + 50% K at fruiting

================================================================================
           SCORING METHODOLOGY FOR BEST CROP RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================

When a farmer asks 'which crops are best for my soil', the system scores all
crops using weighted parameter comparison:

  Parameter      Weight   Logic
  ----------     ------   -----
  Temperature      25     Full penalty if above absolute max; scaled by gap
  pH               20     Scaled by distance from optimal range
  Moisture         20     Scaled by deficit percentage below minimum
  Nitrogen         15     Scaled by deficit percentage below crop minimum
